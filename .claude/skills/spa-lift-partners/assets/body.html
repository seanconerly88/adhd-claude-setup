Questions

Before you book

What happens in the {{CONSULT_MIN}}-minute consult?

You talk with our peptide specialist about your goal and history. If peptides or hormone therapy look like a fit, we schedule your physician visit. If not, we tell you, and that's the end of it.

Is the consult free?

Yes. No cost and no commitment to start anything.

What are peptides?

Short chains of amino acids your body already uses as signals. A protocol uses specific ones to support a specific goal. Your physician explains which, why and what to expect.

What is hormone replacement therapy (HRT)?

HRT replaces or supplements hormones your body may be making less of. Whether it makes sense for you depends on your symptoms, your history and any testing your physician orders. Your physician explains the options, the risks and what to expect.

Who prescribes my protocol?

A licensed physician, after a visit with you. Nothing is prescribed or shipped without that evaluation, and your physician stays available as you go.
